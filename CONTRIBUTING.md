# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in QBS Dev Kit.

## This is a read-only reference repository

QBS Dev Kit is maintained exclusively by [Quantabridges Solutions](https://github.com/Quantabridges-Solutions). It reflects our internal standards, tooling, and conventions as they evolve across our projects.

**We do not accept pull requests or external contributions** to the canonical repository.

## What you can do

- **Fork** this repository for personal use or as a reference baseline
- **Adapt** the templates, rules, and skills to your own team's conventions in your own fork
- **Open an issue** to report a factual error, broken link, or outdated dependency version

## Reporting issues

If you find something incorrect or outdated:

1. Open an [issue](https://github.com/Quantabridges-Solutions/qbs-dev-kit/issues)
2. Describe what is wrong and what the correct behaviour should be
3. We will review and update at our discretion

## Using this kit in your own projects

See the [Getting Started guide](docs/getting-started.md) for full setup instructions.
